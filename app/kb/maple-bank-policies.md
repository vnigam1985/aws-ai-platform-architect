# Maple Leaf Bank customer policies (fictional, for testing only)

## Account opening
To open a chequing account you need one piece of government-issued photo ID and proof of address dated within the last 90 days. Accounts can be opened online or in a branch. Online applications are usually approved within 2 business days. Customers must be at least 18 years old to open an account on their own.

## Monthly fees
The Everyday Chequing account costs $4.95 per month. The fee is waived when the customer keeps a minimum balance of $3,000 for the whole month. The Student account has no monthly fee for customers enrolled full-time at a post-secondary school.

## Overdraft protection
Overdraft protection is optional and must be requested. The overdraft limit is $500 for Everyday Chequing. Interest on overdraft balances is charged at 21% per year, plus a $5 fee for each month the account is overdrawn.

## Lost or stolen cards
Customers must report a lost or stolen debit or credit card immediately by calling 1-800-555-0199, available 24 hours a day, or by locking the card in the mobile app. A replacement card arrives by mail within 5 to 7 business days. Customers are not responsible for unauthorized transactions reported within 2 days of noticing them.

## International transfers
International wire transfers can be sent from online banking. The fee is $30 per transfer. Transfers usually arrive within 1 to 3 business days. The daily limit for international transfers is $10,000. Transfers to sanctioned countries are not allowed.

## Refunds on fees
Customers can ask for a refund of a bank fee within 30 days of the date the fee was charged. Refund requests are reviewed within 5 business days. The bank does not refund interest charges.

## Privacy
The bank never asks for a full password, PIN or one-time passcode by email, text message or phone. Customer data is stored in Canada. Customers can request a copy of their personal information by submitting a privacy request form, and the bank responds within 30 days.

## Branch hours
Most branches are open Monday to Friday from 9:30 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. Selected branches are open on Saturday from 9:30 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. All branches are closed on Sundays and statutory holidays.
